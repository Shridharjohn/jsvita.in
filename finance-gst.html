FAQ

Common questions

Do I need GST registration?

It depends on turnover, the nature of supply and whether you sell inter-state or online. We assess your specific case and register only when required — or when voluntary registration makes commercial sense.

What happens if I miss a return deadline?

Late fees and interest accrue quickly. We bring filings current, set a compliance calendar, and manage due dates so it doesn't repeat.

Can you support a GST audit?

Yes — records review, reconciliations, and coordination with the assessing side, with documentation prepared before it is asked for.
